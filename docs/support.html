Frequently asked

How does the wheel choose a winner?

Each spin uses a uniform random number generator: every segment has exactly the same probability of landing under the pointer, regardless of size.

How is the Yes / No mode weighted?

Yes / No is intentionally not a flat 50/50. We use roughly 45% Yes, 45% No, and 10% Maybe to add a little unpredictability, because "maybe" is often the most honest answer to a real-life question.

Where is my data stored?

Entirely on your device, in a local database (Hive). Diliow does not have a server, an account system, or cloud sync.

Removing the app deletes all of your saved lists, history and settings.

Can I sync my saved lists across devices?

Not yet. Diliow is offline-first by design. We may add an optional cross-device sync in a future major release, but it will always remain opt-in.

How do I clear my decision history?

Open the History screen from the top-right icon on the home page, then tap the trash icon and confirm.

How many options can I add to the wheel or a list?

The wheel supports 2 to 12 segments. Saved lists and the list picker have no hard limit on the number of options.

Can I turn off haptic feedback?

Yes, a setting will be available in an upcoming update. For now, Android's system-wide vibration toggle disables it.

Does Diliow show ads?

No. Diliow is 100% free with no advertising and no in-app purchases.

What languages are supported?

English and French. The app picks your device language automatically; if neither is available, it falls back to English.
