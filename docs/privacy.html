Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 May 2026

Diliow is a decision-maker app that helps you choose between options using a wheel, a coin flip, a list picker or a yes/no question. This privacy policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we collect

None. Diliow does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the following data only on your device, using a local database:

  • Saved lists of options that you create.
  • The 20 most recent decisions you have made.
  • App preferences (onboarding state, haptic feedback toggle).

None of this data leaves your device. You can clear all data at any time by deleting the app from your device. There is no cloud sync, no account, and no backend server.

Third parties

Diliow does not embed any third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

The standard Google Play Console crash reporting that ships with every Android app may collect anonymized crash diagnostics if the user has opted into it at the device level. That data is handled by Google under their own privacy policy.

Permissions

Diliow uses the following Android permissions:

  • VIBRATE — to provide haptic feedback when you spin the wheel, flip the coin or reveal a result.

Children

Diliow is appropriate for users aged 13 and over.

Changes to this policy

If we ever update this policy, we will publish the new version at this URL with an updated "Last updated" date.
